Privacy Policy – Messaging POC

Last updated: August 8, 2026

About this application

This application is a proof of concept demonstrating message delivery through a development interface. It is intended for development, demonstration, and testing purposes.

Information processed

During a test, the service may process:

  • The WhatsApp phone number or identifier of a test participant.
  • Message content sent to the WhatsApp Business test or service number.
  • Basic message metadata, such as the message identifier and time received.

How information is used

Messages are processed only to demonstrate delivery through the test service. Incoming messages may be temporarily stored in the project’s backend database so the development interface can retrieve and display the latest message.

Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to create advertising profiles. Access is limited to the POC and its service environment.

Test-user responsibilities

This is a development application. Test users should not send sensitive, confidential, financial, medical, or other private information through the service.

Data retention and deletion

Test data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and evaluate the POC. Data may be periodically overwritten or deleted. Test participants may request deletion of their test messages by contacting the project owner.
